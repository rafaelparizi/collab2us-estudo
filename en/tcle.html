1. Introduction and invitation

You are invited to take part voluntarily in a pilot study of Collab2Us. Read this form and ask any questions you wish before deciding. The study evaluates the tool and the research procedure; it does not evaluate your competence or your professional performance. Declining or withdrawing will not harm your activities, professional relationships or institutional affiliation.

2. Purpose of the research

The purpose is to identify usability difficulties and opportunities to improve Collab2Us, and to check whether the tasks and instruments are suitable for a later study with students. The tool supports turning ideas from a Miro board into user stories, with quality review, artificial intelligence refinement and export to a Trello backlog, keeping links to the source of the information.

3. Procedures and duration

The pilot involves three teachers, in individual sessions of approximately 30 to 35 minutes. After consent, the researcher will briefly present the tool and an explanatory image. You will carry out six activities with fictitious data: extract ideas from Miro; examine the quality assessment; revise a story with AI support; export it to Trello; check its source; and verify a change or divergence between the records.

The researcher will note the progress of the tasks, the time, difficulties, errors and any requests for help. Afterwards, you will respond to 14 statements about acceptance of the tool, adapted from TAM3 constructs, and to the 10 statements of the SUS usability scale. Finally, three open questions will be asked about contributions, difficulties and improvements. You may decline to answer any question, ask for a break or end the session.

The interview may be recorded in audio only, subject to the specific authorization at the end of this form and to confirmation before recording starts. Declining audio recording does not prevent participation; in that case, answers will be recorded in writing. This form does not authorize video or screen recording.

4. Risks and safeguards

Taking part may cause tiredness, frustration with tool failures or discomfort when reporting difficulties. There is also a risk of undue exposure of information, especially because a voice can identify a person. To reduce these risks, fictitious data and test accounts, codes in the records and restricted access to the data will be used. You will not need to enter real personal or professional information into Miro, Trello or the AI service.

You may ask for help, a break or to end the session. The researcher will stop the recording whenever requested. If discomfort or harm related to your participation occurs, contact the principal investigator for support and appropriate action, without waiving any rights to assistance or compensation.

5. Benefits and expenses

There is no guarantee of direct benefit. Your participation may help improve the tool and the study with students. There will be no payment for participating and no charge for using the tool. Any expenses needed to take part should be discussed with the principal investigator, to ensure they do not fall on you.

6. Privacy and use of results

Records will be treated confidentially. Forms, observations and audio files will receive a participant code. The signed form and the list linking codes to identities will be stored separately. Voice and signature are identifiable; therefore, data collection is not anonymous. Even without names, accounts from a small group may allow indirect recognition, and the team will reduce details that could favor such identification.

The data will be used for scientific and educational purposes, including the undergraduate thesis (TCC), papers and presentations about Collab2Us. Aggregated results and de-identified text excerpts, reviewed to remove personal information, may be published. Names, signatures and audio files will not be published. Individual forms and recordings will not be part of a public artifact package. The SUS analysis will receive only the ten numeric answers, without name or contact details.

7. Storage and withdrawal of data

Access to the records will be restricted to the principal investigator and the authorized collaborator. After collection, the records will be kept in secure storage under the investigator's responsibility for five years after the end of the research. At the end of that period, they will be securely deleted. Temporary copies on recording devices or collection platforms will be removed after the files are transferred and checked.

You may withdraw your consent and request deletion of identifiable records through the investigator's contact. When the data have already been aggregated or irreversibly anonymized, it may not be possible to locate your contribution. Withdrawal will not allow removing results that have already been published. These conditions will be explained to you when you make a request.

8. Questions and copy of the form

You may ask for clarification before, during or after taking part, using the investigator's contact details at the top of this page. For questions or complaints about ethical aspects, the institutional template indicates the Research Ethics Committee of the Federal Institute Farroupilha, email cep@iffar.edu.br.

This form may be signed in two paper copies, one for you and one for the researcher, or electronically, including through gov.br, with a signed copy delivered to you. Data collection will only begin after your consent. Authorization for audio recording will be confirmed again before the interview.

9. Statement of consent

I know that I may decline questions, stop the session or withdraw my consent without any harm. I will receive a copy of this form. By signing, I voluntarily agree to take part in the study and authorize the use of the data under the conditions described.
