Tasks

  1. Extract ideas from Miro. Select the source Miro board, the target Trello board and the Kanban column, and start the extraction.
  2. Examine the quality assessment. In the story editor, look at the QUS score and INVEST indicators for each story.
  3. Revise a story with AI support. Ask for a suggestion, review it, adjust the text if needed and run the assessment again.
  4. Export the story to Trello. Send it to the chosen column and check the export report.
  5. Check the source. Use the source link to go back to the Miro note the story came from.
