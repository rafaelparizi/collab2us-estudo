Step 2 of 2 · Instructions

How the session works

Thank you for agreeing to take part. The session is individual, takes about 30 to 35 minutes and uses only fictitious data and test accounts.

Before you start

  • The study evaluates the tool, not you.
  • Use the test accounts and boards provided by the researcher. Do not enter real personal or professional information into Miro, Trello or the AI service.
  • You can ask for help, take a break or end the session at any time.

Session outline

1. Brief introduction

Collab2Us takes ideas from a Miro board to the Trello backlog in four steps:

  • Step 0 · Integration setup: already done by the researcher; the Miro and Trello accounts are connected.
  • Step 1 · Extraction: extract the notes and relations from the board and build candidate stories.
  • Step 2 · Quality and curation: assess quality with QUS and INVEST, review and, if you wish, ask the AI for suggestions.
  • Step 3 · Export: export the selected stories to Trello, keeping the link to the source notes.

The study focuses on steps 1 to 3. In all of them, you make the decisions, in the role of Product Owner.

2. Five activities you need to do as Product Owner

  1. Extract ideas from Miro and turn them into candidate stories. Select the source Miro board, the target Trello board and the Kanban column, and start the extraction. The tool reads the board's sticky notes and connectors and organizes the ideas into candidate stories for review.
  2. Examine the quality assessment of the stories (QUS score and INVEST indicators). Open the story editor and look at the QUS score and INVEST indicators for each story. They support the review, but they do not replace reading what the story actually asks for.
  3. Revise a story with AI support and decide on the final text. Ask the AI for a suggestion to reword the story, split it or add acceptance criteria in BDD format. Review the suggestion, adjust the text if needed and run the assessment again.
  4. Export the story to Trello. Keep the story selected for export and send it to the chosen Trello column. The export report confirms that the card was created.
  5. Check the source of the exported story on the Miro board. From the exported story, use the source link to go back to the workshop note in Miro that it came from.

3. Questionnaires

You will respond to 14 statements about acceptance of the tool, adapted from TAM3, and to the 10 statements of the SUS usability scale. You may leave any question unanswered.

4. Three open questions

About the tool's contributions, the difficulties you encountered and the improvements you suggest.
